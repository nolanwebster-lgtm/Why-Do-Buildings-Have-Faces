Why Do Buildings Have Faces?

Intro

Over the years I've lived in the East Village, I've wondered about the many unfriendly faces that loom over the storefronts and apartment entryways. There are more throughout the city, but these ones are my neighbors. Carved into brick and stone, they stare unblinking and unmoving, but not indifferent, tracing you with inorganic divots for eyes.

Some are shaped like gods, and some are shaped like monsters. Some you can't tell the difference. Either way, most are shaped like people. I wonder if this, the imitation of a human nature, is why buildings have faces.
